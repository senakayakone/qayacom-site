Lao Keyboard & Stickers – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Lao Keyboard & Stickers is a Lao keyboard with Lao stickers. This page explains what the app does with your data: nothing leaves your phone.

What you type

  • The keyboard does not record, store or send anything you type. It has no internet connection of its own.
  • Full Access (optional) is only used to copy a sticker to the clipboard so you can paste it in a chat.

What we do not do

  • No account, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no sale or sharing of data.
  • When you tap “Add to WhatsApp”, the sticker images are handed to the WhatsApp app on your phone. We receive nothing.
