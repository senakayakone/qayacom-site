Lao Keyboard & Stickers

A Lao keyboard for iPhone, with Lao stickers for WhatsApp and iMessage: ສະບາຍດີ, ຂອບໃຈ, ຮັກເດີ້, 555 and more, still or animated.

Help

  • Turn on the keyboard: Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › Lao Keyboard. Then tap 🌐 in any app to switch to it.
  • Stickers in WhatsApp: in the app, tap “Add to WhatsApp”. Then, in a WhatsApp chat, tap the sticker icon next to the text field and pick “Lao Stickers”.
  • Stickers in iMessage: in Messages, open the app bar under the text field and tap “Lao Stickers”.
  • Stickers in other apps: allow “Full Access” for Lao Keyboard in Settings, tap 😊 on the keyboard, tap a sticker, then paste it in the chat. Full Access is only used to copy the sticker; nothing you type is saved or sent.
