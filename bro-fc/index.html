Bro FC – Weekly Match

Organize your team's weekly football match with your friends: calendar, “I play / I don't play” answers, a drag-and-drop 7-a-side or 8-a-side lineup (white kit vs black kit), ratings and man of the match, a dues table and a team chat. In English, French and Lao.

Help

  • Create a team: open the app, tap “Create a team”, enter the team name and your first name. You get a 6-character code.
  • Join a team: tap “Join a team”, enter the code, then choose your first name in the list made by the admin.
  • New phone? Tap “Continue with Apple” or “Continue with Google” on the Team page beforehand, then sign in again on the new phone to get your place back.
  • Delete my account: Team page, “Delete my account”.
